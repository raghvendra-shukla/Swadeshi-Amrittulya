About Us

Swadeshi Amruttulya is more than just a tea shop — it is a place where tradition meets purity. We believe in serving authentic, hygienic, and freshly prepared tea made from carefully selected ingredients.

Our mission is to revive the true essence of Indian tea culture by offering quality beverages in a warm and welcoming environment. Every cup we serve reflects our commitment to freshness, taste, and customer satisfaction.

I believe that a good cup of tea has the power to refresh the mind and bring people together. Thank you for being a part of our journey and supporting our dream.”
